# Accommodation research — checked 3 November 2026

The guide uses actual displayed quotes for one room, two adults, one night on
Thursday 5, 12, 19 and 26 August **2027**, departing the next day. These are
planning comparisons, not confirmed prices or availability for 24 August 2028.
No booking was made. Rates and availability can change.

| Hotel / room / rate | 5 Aug | 12 Aug | 19 Aug | 26 Aug | Arithmetic mean |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Travelodge Witney / Standard king double / Lowest Rate, room only | £101.99 | £107.99 | £97.99 | £97.99 | £101.49 |
| Blue Boar / Classic Double (sleeps 2) / Flexible Room Only | £118 | £118 | £134 | £120 | £122.50 |
| The Fleece / Cosy Double / Room Only | £129 | £125 | £139 | £133 | £131.50 |
| Double Red Duke / Cosy Room / Bed and Breakfast | £285 | £270 | £270 | £260 | £271.25 |

The website rounds averages to the nearest pound and shows exact samples in
expandable details. Breakfast is excluded from the first three rate sets and
included at the Double Red Duke. Cancellation terms differ; the Travelodge rate
is explicitly non-refundable and non-amendable. Blue Boar quotes use its flexible
rate, not the cheaper saver rate and not its single-occupancy Cosy Double. Taxes
or extras not explicitly confirmed by the booking page are not asserted.

## Primary sources

- [Caswell House local accommodation guide](https://caswellhouse.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Caswell-House-Local-Accommodation.pdf): lists all four hotels, with approximate distances of 3 miles for the Witney hotels and 7.5 miles for the Double Red Duke.
- [Caswell House guest FAQs](https://www.caswellhouse.co.uk/frequently-asked-questions): book taxis in advance; on-site accommodation by invitation from the couple.
- [Travelodge Witney](https://www.travelodge.co.uk/hotels/681/Witney-hotel): rooms, free parking subject to availability, air conditioning and breakfast extra.
- [Travelodge 19 August sample](https://www.travelodge.co.uk/hotels/681/Witney-hotel?checkIn=19%2F08%2F27&checkOut=20%2F08%2F27&rooms%5B0%5D%5Badults%5D=2&rooms%5B0%5D%5Bchildren%5D=0): read rendered room choices, not nearby-hotel recommendations. Other sample dates used the same parameters.
- [Blue Boar rooms](https://www.acornpubs.co.uk/blueboar/bedrooms/) and [19 August booking sample](https://live.high-level-software.com/hotels/bp-the-blue-boar/availability?dateFrom=2027-08-19&dateTo=2027-08-20&numAdults=2&numChildren=0).
- [The Fleece rooms](https://peachpubs.com/rooms/the-fleece/) and [19 August booking sample](https://live.high-level-software.com/hotels/pp-the-fleece/availability?dateFrom=2027-08-19&dateTo=2027-08-20&numAdults=2&numChildren=0).
- [Double Red Duke, Clanfield](https://www.countrycreatures.com/double-red-duke/) and [19 August booking sample](https://booking.eu.guestline.app/CCR/availability?hotel=DBREDDUKE&arrival=2027-08-19&departure=2027-08-20&adults=2&children=0): verified displayed check-in/out inputs for all four sample dates.

The price pages require JavaScript; plain HTTP responses sometimes omit rates.
The Minster Mill booking engine denied the request, so no price was inferred.
Google returned an automated-traffic challenge; no search snippets were used as
rate evidence. Recommendations rely on the venue and hotel websites above.
